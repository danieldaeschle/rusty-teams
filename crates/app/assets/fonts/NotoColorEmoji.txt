Noto Color Emoji (CBDT/CBLC) from
https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/tree/main/2D/fonts

Rusty Teams adds a cmap entry for U+006D, mapped onto an existing glyph, so
GPUI keeps the face when a text run asks for this family. The family name
"Noto Color Emoji" and the PostScript name "NotoColorEmoji" are unchanged.
